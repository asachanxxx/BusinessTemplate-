🔷 Core Sections (Almost every site MUST have)
Header (Top Bar) 👉 Logo, navigation, CTA (Call / Book / Contact)
Hero Section 👉 Hero Carousel with many images,  Big headline, value proposition, CTA
Services / Offerings 👉 What the business provides (cards or list)
Booking / Appointment Section 👉 For services
About / Trust Section 👉 Who they are, experience, why trust them
Testimonials / Reviews 👉 Customer feedback (very important for SMB)
Call-To-Action (CTA) Section 👉 “Book now”, “Get quote”, “Contact us”
Contact Section 👉 Form, phone, email, address
Footer 👉 Links, contact info, social, legal

🔷 High-Impact Sections (Strongly Recommended)
Gallery / Portfolio 👉 Real work (before/after, projects)
Pricing / Packages 👉 Clear offers (optional but powerful)
FAQ 👉 Reduce customer doubts
Map / Location 👉 Especially for local businesses
Partners / Certifications 👉 Trust badges, logos
